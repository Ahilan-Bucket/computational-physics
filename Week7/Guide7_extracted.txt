Introduction 
This week we will look at solving partial differential equations (PDEs) that give solutions that depend on 
several variables.  Common physics examples include the 1D wave equation, where the amplitude of the 
wave depends on both position x and time t, or Laplace’s equation for the electrostatic potential which 
depends on position (e.g., x, y, and z). 
The topic of partial differential equations and how to solve them both analytically and numerically is 
vast.  By doing several applications, you will learn a few of the basic techniques for solving different 
classes of PDEs.   
But first, let’s go over some notation.  For the PDEs that follow, we will have functions that are just

The three types of equations we will consider in the problems will be of the form 
𝑢𝑥𝑥+ 𝑢𝑦𝑦= 0          (Laplace’s equation)
